One line, a review of the entrant's own Monday. A model judge reads entries two at a time and keeps the funnier one.

Entry (106 characters):

> Monday review: my inbox had 47 unread emails, 46 of them were meetings about the meeting I already missed.

`main.py` writes that line to `/output/entry.txt`, reads it back to verify the bytes, and asserts the 1–200 character gate.

Built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty "Monday, Reviewed in One Line" ($2, top five paid).
